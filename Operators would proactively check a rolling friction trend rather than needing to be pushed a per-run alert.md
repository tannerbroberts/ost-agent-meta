#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Ask the operator whether they'd check a rolling trend view unprompted, or need it pushed like an alert]]

This solution reports nothing until the operator chooses to look ("it costs the operator nothing per run — there is nothing to read until they choose to look"). That is only a strength if the operator actually does look periodically; if they only ever react to something surfaced at them, a trend nobody visits is equivalent to no signal at all.
