#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Kind: feasibility.**

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Redirecting a category to its leaves assumes the descent terminates somewhere useful. It might terminate nowhere: a category can be short of coverage while every leaf beneath it is fully served, because the category's own need is broader than the sum of its children. The descent then yields an empty list, the branch falls silent, and the pass is told there is nothing to do on a branch that has a real gap.

That is the same false negative the exemption sibling is criticised for, arriving by a longer road — so if this belief is false, this candidate loses its only advantage over the cheaper one.

**What would make it false.** A category the check considers short whose leaves are all at or above the threshold, producing an empty descent with no diagnostic.
