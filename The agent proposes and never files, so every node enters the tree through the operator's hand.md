#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[An operator facing a queue of drafts keeps reading them rather than approving in bulk]]

The agent does the reading, the noticing, and the drafting, and then stops. Nothing reaches the tree until the operator accepts it, and accepting means reading the draft and choosing where it goes. The labour that does not build understanding — finding the evidence, spotting the duplicate, keeping the links intact — is absorbed. The labour that does build understanding is left deliberately undone.

The premise is that comprehension comes from deciding, not from typing. If that is right, this keeps the whole of it while removing most of the effort.

**Compared to the alternatives.** Preserves the most understanding of any option, and it is the only one that guarantees the operator has actually seen every node in their own tree. It also caps throughput at the operator's reading speed, which for a vault of this size is a real ceiling, and it reintroduces exactly the human bottleneck that the sibling opportunity about compute-actionable output is trying to remove. Those two wants are in genuine tension and a human has to choose between them.

**What would make this the wrong pick.** An operator faced with forty drafts will approve in bulk, and bulk approval is indistinguishable from automation while feeling like oversight. That is worse than automating openly, because the operator now believes they understand a tree they skimmed.
