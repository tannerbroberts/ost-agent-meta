[[Replay past sweeps to see how many were blind all the way rather than partly]]

Refusing a zero-subject sweep is worth doing if blindness is usually total. If past sweeps were mostly partly-blind — a real subject list with gaps in it — then the empty-subject case is rare and this catches the least common shape of the problem.
