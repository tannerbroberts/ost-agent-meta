#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Count this vault's dated claims by age and require the expired share to fall between 10 and 70 percent]]

**Feasibility belief about this vault's own shape, not about the mechanism:** the dated measured claims on this tree are spread across enough different ages that marking the old ones actually partitions them.

A label is only information if it discriminates. If nearly every dated claim here is old — and this tree is dense with first-party observations from single passes that nothing will ever re-take — then at any plausible shelf life almost everything reads "not measured since", and a reader learns exactly what they knew before opening the node. That is the unlabelled state with more words, which this candidate's own body names as its risk.

The failure runs both ways and both are worth stating, because they argue for opposite shelf lives and one of them is the trap. **Too much expired** and the label is noise. **Too little** and it never fires, so the mechanism costs a field and buys nothing — and this is the trap, because a label that never fires looks exactly like a label that is working. Neither reading can be taken from the mechanism; both are properties of the corpus, and both are countable today over the vault as it stands.

What this assumption deliberately does not cover: whether an operator changes what they do on seeing the label. That is a different belief and a person is the only measurement of it.
