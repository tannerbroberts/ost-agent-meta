#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that buyers recognise their own discipline as the problem. Nobody thinks they are the one who overclaims, so the pitch may describe a weakness the listener does not believe is theirs — and that framing risk, not whether the mechanisms exist, is what decides this.

**Risk category: desirability.**

**Design.** Ask ten practitioners about a specific past occasion: tell me about a time you shipped something on evidence that turned out to be thinner than you thought. Do not mention the tool, the mechanisms, or discipline. Count how many produce a real instance without prompting, and how many attribute it to their own process rather than to circumstance.

**Why it is small.** Ten conversations about things that already happened, and the question can ride along with other interviews.

**What it will not cover.** Willingness to admit a past lapse in conversation is not willingness to buy a tool that assumes it. This measures recognition, which is necessary and well short of sufficient.

A human runs this and records the result.
