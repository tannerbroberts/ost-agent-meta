#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that solo builders feel the absence of a sceptic as a problem. The tool's own vault is a sample of one and that one is the author, so generalising from it is exactly the move the tool exists to prevent.

**Risk category: desirability.**

**Design.** Ten story-based interviews with people building alone. Ask about the last significant thing they decided to build: what made them confident, who if anyone pushed back, and what happened. Do not describe the tool. Count how many describe wanting a check they did not have, and how many describe having sought one out.

**Why it is small.** Ten conversations about past behaviour, no product involved, and it can share recruiting with the other segment tests.

**What it will not cover.** Wanting a sceptic is not wanting one made of software, and someone who says they wish they had a colleague may not accept a tool that refuses them. It also cannot reach the segment's willingness to pay, which is separately doubtful.

A human runs this and records the result.
