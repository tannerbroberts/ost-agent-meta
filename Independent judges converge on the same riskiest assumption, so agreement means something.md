#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Have three independent judges nominate for ten solutions and see whether they agree with each other]]

The mechanism reads agreement as corroboration. If judges scatter — each naming a different risk — then agreement when it occurs is coincidence, and a second model with no independent grounding produces the appearance of corroboration, which is more dangerous than an openly self-assessed gate.
