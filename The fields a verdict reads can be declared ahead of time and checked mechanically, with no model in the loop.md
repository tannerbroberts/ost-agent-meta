#Assumption #compression #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Declare each bounded surface's decision-fields contract and mechanically verify the bounded output preserves it]]

The fidelity instrument only works if "what the decision reads" is declarable: that for each bounded surface, the downstream verdict consumes an enumerable set of fields (counts, statuses, flags, marked spans) rather than an irreducible gestalt of the whole body. If any surface's decision turns out to depend on content no declared contract can name, that surface cannot be scored model-free and the instrument silently under-measures it. Stated so it could be wrong: every compression surface in this product serves a decision whose inputs can be written down as a contract, and a mechanical check of the bounded output against that contract is a faithful proxy for "the decision survives".
