#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #desirability

**Assumption under test (desirability):** Goal drift is a fear operators actually hold, rather than one the founder has projected onto them.

**Proposed test:** Ask five people who would plausibly run an autonomous agent overnight: "tell me about the last time you left an automated process running unsupervised — what did you check on, and what were you afraid of?" Story-based and past-behaviour; do not mention goals, drift, or scope.

**Size:** five twenty-minute conversations, no build.

**Pre-committed threshold:** ≥3 of 5 raise goal, scope, or direction drift unprompted. If nobody does, the locking mechanism solves a founder worry and the compute belongs on crash-safety or liveness instead.

**Decides:** whether the trust worry is about halting, corruption, or drift — three siblings, only one of which the evidence should fund first.

Proposed by the agent — to be run by a human with real operators; the questions must not lead. No results recorded here.
