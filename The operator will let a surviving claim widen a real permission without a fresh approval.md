#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Ask the operator to name one permission class a survived stake may widen with nobody watching]]

**Risk category: viability.** The ledger's whole value is that survived stakes convert into wider permission *without* a human at the moment of widening — otherwise it is just a log in front of the same approval queue. This could be false: an operator may happily read the ledger yet still insist on approving every widening by hand, in which case the ledger changes what they see but not what the agent may do, and the opportunity above ("earn wider autonomy") is not served.
