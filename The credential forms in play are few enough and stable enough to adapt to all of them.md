[[Count the distinct credential forms in play and how often each changed in a year]]

Accepting many forms means handling many forms, and the safest thing a tool can do with a secret is not touch it. The trade only works if the set is small and does not churn — otherwise this maximises exposure in exchange for convenience that keeps breaking.
