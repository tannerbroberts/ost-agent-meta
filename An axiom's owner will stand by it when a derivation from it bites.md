#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Present the founder three derivations from their own axioms, one unwelcome, and count silent revocations]]

**Risk category: desirability/viability.** The proof lane only produces evidence if declared axioms behave like commitments: when a machine-checked derivation reaches an unwelcome conclusion, the axiom's owner either accepts the conclusion or revises the axiom in writing. If axioms get silently revoked whenever a derivation bites, the lane is a rubber stamp — every conclusion anyone wanted was "derivable" and none of it is evidence. This could be false: the founder themselves named the axiom distinction as "really very felt-out," which may mean axioms are contextual judgements that resist being frozen into a register at all.
