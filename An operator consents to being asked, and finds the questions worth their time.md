[[Withhold the agent's answer on ten forks and measure how often the operator diverges]]

An agent withholding its answer to test its operator is doing something the operator has to have agreed to. An operator in a hurry experiences it as being quizzed by their own tooling — so consent and frequency both have to be theirs, and the questions have to be ones they are glad were asked.
