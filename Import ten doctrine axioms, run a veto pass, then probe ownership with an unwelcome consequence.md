#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Small, fast test of one belief.** Extract ten candidate axioms from this vault's own recorded doctrine (DEC records, the ruleset — a body the founder already trusts). Run a veto-only pass: strike or keep, no rewording. Then probe ownership: present one unwelcome-but-valid consequence derived from a surviving axiom and record the response.

**Pass:** at least six survivors endorsed as genuinely theirs, and the unwelcome consequence is met with acceptance or a written revision of the specific axiom. **Fail:** survivors are shrugged at ("I guess I didn't object") or the probe is answered by disowning the imported set — veto-only review does not manufacture ownership, and this solution loses to authoring or lazy elicitation.

**What this does not settle.** One body of practice, one operator; nothing about bodies external to the vault, and nothing about how imported and authored axioms would coexist in one register.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The founder is irreducibly the measurement: felt ownership of a vetoed-not-authored axiom is their reaction, observable only by staging the review and the probe with them.
