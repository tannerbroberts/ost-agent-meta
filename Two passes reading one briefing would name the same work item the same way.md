#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** A claim only excludes a second pass if the second pass recognises the claim as covering the work it was about to start. That requires two independent readings of one briefing to produce the same item identity.

It could easily be false, and the observed collision is the reason to doubt it rather than to believe it. Two passes read one briefing paragraph and converged on nearly identical *code* — same migration number, same column name, same hash function — which is convergence on implementation, not on a name. Nothing in that record shows either pass ever articulated what it was working on as a discrete, nameable item. If two readings produce "invited-visitor arm split" and "add arm column to visitor_events", a claim on the first does not stop the second.

**Why this is the riskiest assumption under this solution.** The mechanics of writing and reading a claim file are trivial and nobody doubts them. Everything this candidate promises rests on the vocabulary holding, and the vocabulary is the part no one has looked at.

⚠️ Unvalidated, agent-authored, and the record it reasons from is a single collision observed by this building's own loop.
