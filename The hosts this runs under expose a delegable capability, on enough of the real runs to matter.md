[[Enumerate the hosts this tool runs under and check which expose a delegable capability]]

Borrowing the host's authority also inherits its scope, which is usually far wider than needed. An operator happy to issue a narrow token may be much less happy to let a background pass act as their whole signed-in self — and that is before asking whether the hosts expose delegation at all.
