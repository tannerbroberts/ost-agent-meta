#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Replay the corpus to count how many correct path guesses the guard would have taxed]]

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Requiring a look before every path-taking command costs fewer turns than the wrong guesses it prevents.

**Why it is the riskiest one.** Most guesses are right. The corpus records the failures and is silent about the far larger number of path-taking calls that worked first time — and this guard taxes every one of those to catch the minority that did not. The solution's own body concedes it is the read-before-write handshake generalised, and that handshake is this vault's single most frequent friction event at roughly twenty collisions across eleven sessions. Proposing more of the mechanism that hurts most needs a number behind it, not an argument.

**What class this is.** Feasibility and cost, answerable by replaying the corpus. Not a customer question.

**How it could come out false — and it might well.** If correct guesses outnumber wrong ones ten to one, the guard adds nine listings to save one failure and should be dropped in favour of its cheaper sibling. That would be a clean refutation and it is available for the price of a count.
