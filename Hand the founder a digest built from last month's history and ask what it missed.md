#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

Small and fast: an attended session compiles one digest by hand from the last month of vault history (the 20 recorded red-to-green observations and 4 was-already-green records are the known raw material), hands it to the founder, and asks two questions — what here would you have wanted pushed to you, and what happened last month that this misses? No code is built; the digest is compiled once, manually. This is comparative evidence for choosing between digest and criteria-note: every missed class the founder names is a criteria-note entry the history does not yet record.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: Only the founder can say whether the highlights he wanted are in the digest — the question is what he meant, and no repository or spec can answer for him.
