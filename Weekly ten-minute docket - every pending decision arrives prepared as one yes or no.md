#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Time one real week of decisions through the docket from a phone]]

**The idea.** The agent maintains a standing docket: each item is one decision, pre-chewed to a single yes/no with a three-sentence context, the evidence link, and the exact command that enacts each answer. Promotions, kills, gate overrides, test verdicts, outreach approvals — all of it. The operator's entire weekly involvement is one ten-minute read-and-answer session, from a phone.

**Contrast with siblings:** the triage sibling shrinks WHAT needs a human; this shrinks the COST of what remains. Extends /ost-review rather than competing with it.

**Trade-off:** pre-chewing is framing power — the docket author steers the disposer. Mitigation: every item links the raw evidence, and the docket is append-only so framing is auditable after the fact.
