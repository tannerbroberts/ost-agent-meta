#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Every quoting surface renders the whole sentence, not just the detector that already does]]

A guarantee that holds on one detector and not the others is not a guarantee — a reader who learns to trust the full-sentence rendering will extend that trust to surfaces that never adopted it. The property has to be universal across quoting surfaces or it is worse than absent.
