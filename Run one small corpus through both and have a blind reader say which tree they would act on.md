#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that the comparison comes out favourably. The funded tools may produce richer, more confident, better-written trees, and a reader who does not already care about grounding will read that as this tool losing — which makes publishing a comparison nobody controls the reading of a real risk.

**Risk category: desirability.** Run this before publishing anything, not after.

**Design.** Take one small corpus of real customer evidence. Run it through this tool and through one funded competitor. Strip both trees of anything identifying which produced them. Give both to five readers who fit the target buyer and ask which they would act on, and why. Count.

**Why it is small.** One corpus, two runs, five readers, nothing published.

**What it will not cover.** Five readers on one corpus is a small sample, and the corpus is chosen by the person hoping for a result. It also cannot say whether the tree that reads better is the one that was right.

A human runs this and records the result.
