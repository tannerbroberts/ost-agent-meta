#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Write the gap as an unknown and see whether a Format can be stated for it at all]]

Framing the gap as darkness requires it to be an unknown. If the reason the real goal cannot be charted is money or time, a constraint has been dressed as an unknown — which buys a tidy entry and no progress. Whether a Format can be stated without reducing to a resource claim is the test of which it is.
