#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #desirability

**Assumption under test (desirability):** Teams outside the founder's immediate network will hand real discovery work — their outcome, their customer evidence — to a stranger's agent.

**Proposed test:** Offer a free, done-for-you discovery pass to ~20 qualified teams (people who already run discovery) through direct outreach. Ask for a 45-minute kickoff and their real artefacts. Measure who says yes and who actually sends material.

**Size:** about a week of outreach, no build required.

**Pre-committed threshold (set before running):** ≥5 of 20 book the kickoff AND ≥3 send real artefacts within two weeks. Below that, the concierge path is not viable at any scale and attention should move to a sibling.

**Decides:** whether Concierge is a live option relative to the public trial and the interview habit.

Proposed by the agent — to be run by a human with real people. No results recorded here.
