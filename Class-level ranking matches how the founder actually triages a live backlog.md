#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Run one live triage offering only class-rank verbs and watch for per-node fallback]]

**Risk category: desirability.** The directive asks for class-level rank verbs, but asking for a verb and reaching for it under load are different facts. This belief is that, given a real triage moment over this tree's own backlog, the founder ranks *classes* — and the class boundaries the tree can draw are the ones they mean. It could be false two ways: they may fall back to per-node judgements the moment a class contains one node they feel differently about, or the classes the tree can express (today: the category buckets) may not be the classes in their head.
