# Cold-offer outreach kit — "will outside teams hand over real discovery work?"

Companion to the assumption test `Cold-offer test - will outside teams hand over real
discovery work`. Pre-committed threshold (do not move it after sending): **≥5 of 20
book the kickoff AND ≥3 send real artefacts within two weeks** — below that, the
concierge path dies and attention moves to a sibling.

Every reply — including every "no" and every silence — goes into
`.ost-agent/inbox/` as evidence. The noes are the first external data this vault
will ever hold; do not cherry-pick.

## Who counts as qualified (pick 20 before sending anything)

People who *already run discovery*, not people who might like the idea:
- PMs/founders who mention continuous discovery, Teresa Torres, opportunity solution
  trees, or weekly customer interviews in public (LinkedIn posts, blog, podcasts,
  Product Talk community, #product Slack/Discord groups)
- Discovery/research coaches with client teams
- Solo founders who post build-in-public discovery notes

Disqualify: personal friends, anyone who already knows about OST-Agent from you
(the point is OUTSIDE the founder's network), anyone with no evidence of an
existing discovery habit.

Log all 20 in a sheet before the first send: name, source, date sent, reply,
kickoff booked?, artefacts received? — the sheet itself becomes the test result.

## The offer (what you're actually proposing)

A free, done-for-you discovery pass: they hand over their desired outcome and
whatever raw evidence they have (interview notes, support tickets, survey dumps);
you return a structured opportunity solution tree in Obsidian — opportunities
distilled from *their* evidence, candidate solutions, and small pre-committed
assumption tests — within a week. 45-minute kickoff, that's their whole cost.

## Email / DM draft (~120 words, adjust voice, don't soften the ask)

Subject: I'll build your opportunity solution tree from your real evidence — free

Hi {name} — I saw {specific thing: their post on weekly interviews / their OST
screenshot / their discovery podcast episode}.

I'm testing a tool that turns raw discovery material — interview notes, tickets,
survey exports — into a maintained opportunity solution tree (Torres-style:
opportunities from your evidence, competing solutions, small assumption tests,
everything marked unvalidated for you to judge).

I'd like to run one real pass on your actual discovery work, free. You'd give me
a 45-minute kickoff and whatever raw material you're comfortable sharing; you get
the tree back in Obsidian within a week, yours to keep either way.

Interested? Happy to sign an NDA for the raw material.

{signature}

## Handling replies

- **Yes** → book kickoff inside 5 days; artefacts received = the second counter.
- **"Tell me more"** → one reply max, then the ask again; a second deflection is a no.
- **No / silence after one bump at day 4** → record verbatim reason if given. The
  reasons are opportunity-layer gold — file each in the inbox individually.

## After two weeks

File the sheet + all replies in `.ost-agent/inbox/`, run a pass so the evidence
enters the tree, and record the threshold verdict in the assumption test's node —
the first human-run test result in this vault's history.
