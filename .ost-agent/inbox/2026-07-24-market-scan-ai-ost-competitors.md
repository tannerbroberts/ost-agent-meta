# Market scan: the AI-assisted OST space is already being productized (2026-07-24)

Found during cold-offer candidate sourcing (compute sweep, public web). Evidence class:
observed market artifacts — real third-party products and content, mechanically
discoverable; NOT demand evidence for OST-Agent.

- **Perspective AI** publishes 2026 guides on feeding an opportunity solution tree with
  always-on AI-moderated customer interviews ("the biggest failure mode in 2026 is
  starving the OST") — an adjacent competitor automating the *evidence supply* side
  where OST-Agent automates the *tree maintenance* side.
  https://getperspective.ai/blog/opportunity-solution-tree-2026-practical-guide-continuous-discovery
- **CraftUpLearn** ships a free web OST builder; **The Product Courier** (Substack)
  published "Build an Opportunity Solution Tree in One Click" — AI-generated OSTs are
  becoming a content-marketing commodity.
- Vendor content teams (Product School, Koji, Mambo, Askable, Maze, GreatQuestion,
  Chameleon) all maintain OST explainers — heavy SEO competition on the niche's terms.

**Why filed:** direct evidence for the open opportunity "I can't say why anyone wouldn't
just do this by hand with Claude and Obsidian" — the sharper 2026 version is "why
wouldn't they use one of these funded tools?" OST-Agent's visible differentiators against
this field: append-only trust architecture, believability-ladder honesty, local/operator-
owned vaults, and the proposer/disposer boundary. None of the scanned competitors
advertise anything in that territory.
