Founder-stated opportunity (founder-origin; unvalidated). "I want my autonomous agent to be hungry for a real-world product merely because real users are real data, and that grounds the product as opposed to a model's or founder's signoff." The scarce input is external, non-founder data (demand and usage). Today there are only two input channels — the agent (via its own dogfooding friction) and the founder — and neither is an outside user. Willingness-to-pay and retention are the highest-signal data and are entirely absent.
