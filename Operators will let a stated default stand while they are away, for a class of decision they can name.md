#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Ask five operators whether they would let a stated default stand while they are away]]

Making the operator's cheapest action doing nothing requires their consent to silence-as-consent. The boundary between what may proceed on a default and what may not is the hard problem, and it is only tractable if operators name overlapping boundaries rather than five different ones.
