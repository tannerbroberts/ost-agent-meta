#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Kind: feasibility.**

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Ageing treats "no pass acted on it" as evidence that no pass needs to. The parent already argues the opposite — items went unmapped because the surface had no way to discharge them, not because they were uninteresting. If that is right, then the item most likely to age out is not the most redundant one but the one whose disposition is hardest, and hardest is often novel.

**What would make it false.** Any item in the current backlog that is genuinely novel — bearing on no existing opportunity — and old enough that this rule would have aged it out.
