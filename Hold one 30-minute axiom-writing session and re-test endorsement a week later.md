#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Small, fast test of one belief.** Schedule one 30-minute session in which the founder writes axioms they accept about this product's world, blank page. One week later, read each axiom back paired with one concrete consequence a derivation could draw from it, and ask: still yours?

**Pass:** at least five axioms written, at least four of five re-endorsed unchanged under a stated consequence. **Fail:** the session doesn't produce five, or endorsements collapse when consequences are attached — evidence the felt-out distinction resists advance codification and the register would fill with shelf axioms.

**What this does not settle.** One author, one week: nothing about revision mechanics, nothing about whether derivations can be machine-checked, and a pass does not validate the register solution — it only removes its sharpest desirability doubt.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The founder is irreducibly the measurement: whether they will author axioms and still endorse them later is their behavior, not a repository fact.
