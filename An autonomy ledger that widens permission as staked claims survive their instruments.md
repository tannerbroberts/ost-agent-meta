#Solution #founder-theory #goal-acquisition #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[The operator will let a surviving claim widen a real permission without a fresh approval]]
[[A survived claim is distinguishable in the record from one whose spec was never written]]

Make "grows its autonomy as it proves it deserves it" a mechanism instead of a mood. A ledger records every claim the agent staked, the instrument that judged it, and the outcome; permission scope is a *function of the ledger* — sustained survived-claims widen what the agent may probe or spend unattended, refuted claims narrow it, and the mapping from track record to scope is human-set the way the spend ceiling is. Trust stops being a constant configured once and becomes a price reality has been paid.

**Contrast with siblings:** the other two limbs generate verdicts (empirically, or by derivation); this one *consumes* verdicts and converts them into scope. It is inert without at least one of them, which is a real dependency and recorded here as one.

**Known failure mode, named at birth:** the agent choosing only easy claims to farm the ledger. Any build needs the widening judged against claim difficulty or claim usefulness, not claim count — otherwise this is the "agent narrows its own capability to get past a gate I set" pain, inverted.

**Provenance caveat:** founder-directed mapping (2026-08-10), floor rung (`assertion`).

## Definition of done

"The ledger credits a red-then-green claim and credits nothing for a no-spec-only history"

```
npx vitest run test/eval/autonomy-ledger-credit.test.ts
```

Red today because no ledger exists and the spec is unwritten; green when one exists and refuses to credit an unmeasured claim. Written blind of an existing spec, so its first observation files as `no-spec` — the bar above is what carries a builder, per `confirmPermit`'s `thresholdBound` rule.

**Why this is the first thing to build here, ahead of the widening itself.** This node names "the agent choosing only easy claims to farm the ledger" as a failure mode at birth. On this product that is not a tendency to design against — it is the default, because the only instrument an agent surface can author is one naming a spec nobody has written, and those file as `no-spec`. A ledger that counts claims before it can tell a measured one from an unmeasured one is farmable by construction, using artefacts the claimant mints at will.

**This command settles feasibility only**, and only the crediting half. Whether an operator will let any track record widen a real permission unattended is the sibling assumption — "Ask the operator to name one permission class a survived stake may widen with nobody watching" — and it stays a person's. Claim *difficulty*, the other half of this node's own caution, is untouched: a green here stops the cheapest claims counting and says nothing about whether the rest are hard.
