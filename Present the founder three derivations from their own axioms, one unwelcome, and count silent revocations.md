#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Small, fast test of one belief.** Ask the founder to write down five axioms they accept about this product's world. A facilitator (or attended session) prepares three short derivations that follow from those axioms alone, choosing at least one whose conclusion the founder is known to dislike. Present each and record the reaction.

**Pass:** every derivation is either accepted as binding or answered with a written revision of the specific axiom it followed from. **Fail:** any conclusion is rejected while its axioms are left standing — that is the silent revocation that would make a proof lane a rubber stamp.

**What this does not settle.** A pass grounds desirability/viability of axiom-keeping for one person; it says nothing about whether derivations can be machine-checked (feasibility, a separate assumption) or whether anyone beyond the founder would use a proof lane.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The founder is irreducibly the measurement: whether they stand by an axiom under an unwelcome conclusion is their behavior, not a repository fact.
