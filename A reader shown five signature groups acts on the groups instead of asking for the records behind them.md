#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Show one operator the grouped queue and the record listing, and record which one they act from]]

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** An operator (or a firing) shown "463 records, 5 distinct signatures" treats that as five pieces of work and acts on them, rather than treating the grouping as a summary to be verified and going back to the individual records anyway.

It could easily be false. A grouping computed by a machine, stored nowhere, and explained by nothing is exactly the kind of claim a careful reader distrusts — and the candidate's own give-up is that a wrong merge leaves no trace. A reader who does not trust the grouping pays for it twice: once to read the groups and again to read the records. In that case the candidate has added cost and removed nothing.

**Risk category: desirability, bordering usability.** Whether the grouping is computable is not in doubt and is not what this measures; whether it changes what anybody does is the whole question.

**Why no spec can answer this.** The exit code can prove that groups are emitted. It cannot prove that seeing them changed a decision, and "the reader believed it" is not a property of the code. This needs a person put in front of both renderings.
