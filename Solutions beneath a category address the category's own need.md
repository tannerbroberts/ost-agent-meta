#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[A category whose subtree is full stops being reported as under-served]]

**Kind: feasibility** (with a desirability shadow, noted below).

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Counting a category as served because its subtree holds solutions assumes those solutions collectively cover the category. They might not. A category with five sub-opportunities, where 45 solutions sit under one of them and zero under the other four, would read as amply served while four fifths of its need has nothing.

If that shape is common in this tree, rollup does not fix the miscount — it inverts it, and the inverted version is worse because it is silent.

**What would make it false.** Any current category whose subtree solution count clears the threshold while a majority of its leaf sub-opportunities carry none.
