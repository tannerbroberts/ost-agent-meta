#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Test does a 10 percent sample estimate whole-tree quality]]

Sampling is what makes human ground truth affordable. It only works if the sample generalises — if quality varies by branch or by age, a tenth of the nodes gives a number that moves for sampling reasons rather than quality ones.
