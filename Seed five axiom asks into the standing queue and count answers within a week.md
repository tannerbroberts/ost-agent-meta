#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Small, fast test of one belief.** Draft five realistic axiom asks (each: one axiom a plausible derivation would need, one accept/reject question, the consequence of accepting). Place them in the operator's standing ask queue at the start of a normal week — no reminders, no chasing. Count answers at day seven.

**Pass:** four or more answered accept-or-reject within the week, each in under two minutes by the operator's own report. **Fail:** asks age unanswered — lazy elicitation would block derivations exactly the way the founder's frustration describes, and the queue-flood risk is real at even five items.

**What this does not settle.** Five seeded asks are not the fan-out of real theory work; a pass says the cadence can absorb a trickle, not a flood, and says nothing about the quality of the accepted axioms.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The operator is irreducibly the measurement: whether they clear small asks at their own cadence is their observed behavior over a real week.
