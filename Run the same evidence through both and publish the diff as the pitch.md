#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

Take one real corpus of customer evidence, put it through this tool and through each funded competitor, and publish all the trees side by side with the corpus. Let a reader see what each one made of the same material — where they agree, what one found that the others missed, and how many claims in each are backed by something a sceptic would accept.

The pitch becomes an artefact rather than a position, and the comparison is one anyone can rerun.

**Compared to the alternatives.** The only option that could be wrong in public, which is exactly what makes it persuasive if it goes well. Refusal-based positioning and file-ownership positioning are both assertions a competitor can counter-assert; a published diff on shared input is much harder to wave away. The cost is that it takes real work, needs a corpus that can be published, and hands competitors a free evaluation harness pointed at their own weaknesses and yours.

**What would make this the wrong pick.** The diff may show the funded tools producing richer, more confident, better-written trees — and a reader who does not already care about grounding will read that as this tool losing. Publishing a comparison you do not control the interpretation of is a real risk, not a rhetorical one.

This describes a comparison a human would run and publish. No pass may run it or record its result.
