#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

Isolating every firing in its own worktree removes cross-firing contamination only if the harness's actual firing cadence and disk budget can absorb the added setup/teardown cost. At high firing frequency, worktree creation itself could become the bottleneck or the disk-exhaustion risk it was meant to avoid.
