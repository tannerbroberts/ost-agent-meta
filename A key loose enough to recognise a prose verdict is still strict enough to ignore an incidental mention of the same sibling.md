#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[A prose verdict naming the rule and the sibling clears the flag, an incidental mention does not, and a verdict on one relation kind does not carry to another]]

**Kind: feasibility — and it is the whole solution.** The belief: an Issues line can be recognised as a verdict on a specific pair by its rule name plus the quoted sibling title, and that same rule does not fire on the other ways a sibling gets quoted in prose — a corroboration note, a "distinct from its sibling …" cross-reference, a differentia statement. Stated so it could be false: this vault's own nodes quote siblings constantly (every extent verdict written so far names two or three), and if the loose key matches any of those non-verdict mentions, a real flag is silenced by a sentence that never judged it.

**Second thing it must hold, or the solution is unsafe:** a verdict on one relation kind must not carry to another — `shared-extent` judged DISTINCT on 2026-08-11 must not silence a `subset-extent` flag on the same pair raised after new evidence landed.

**Uncovered by any spec:** whether a pass writes "MERGE" and then does not merge, silencing the flag with an unkept promise. That is an observation over future firings, not an assertion.
