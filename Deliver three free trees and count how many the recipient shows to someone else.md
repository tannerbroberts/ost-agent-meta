#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that the tree travels. Free work delivered to a busy team often goes unread, and an unread tree reaches nobody — the whole mechanism depends on the recipient caring enough to show someone, which is the step furthest outside anyone's control.

**Risk category: desirability.**

**Design.** Deliver three trees to teams outside the network. Do not ask them to share it. Four weeks later, ask what they did with it: who else saw it, whether it came up in any review, whether anything changed because of it. Count trees that reached a second person.

**Why it is small.** Three deliveries, three follow-up conversations. The offer itself doubles as a demand test, since a team that will not hand over real evidence has already answered something.

**What it will not cover.** Three is a very small sample and the trees will be built with unusual care. It also measures whether the artefact travels, not whether anyone would pay for it.

A human runs this and records the result.
