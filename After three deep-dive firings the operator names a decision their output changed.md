#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Small and pre-committed:** run three deep-dive firings on branches the operator picks, then ask one question — "did any of these change something you decided?" — and record the answer. The bar is deliberately one of three, because the failure being guarded against (twenty passes of commentary) scored zero, and a single changed decision already beats the recorded baseline.

**What this does not settle:** the exchange rate against sweep firings — whether the changed decision was worth the firings spent. That is a viability comparison for after the mechanism exists at all.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: Whether an analysis changed a decision is a fact about the operator's decisions; only they can report it.
