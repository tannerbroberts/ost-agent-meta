#AssumptionTest #machine-witness #goal-acquisition #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

One small, fast test of one belief: that clearing the gate on machine-witnessed evidence also clears the way to *act* on the permit unattended.

**Design.** Put the question to the founder directly, in one sitting, as a decision rather than a discussion: "When the gate clears a solution on a machine-witnessed verdict, may the unattended loop start building it before you have seen the permit — yes, no, or yes-for-some-classes (name them)?" Record the answer verbatim on this node.

**Why this is irreducibly human.** The doctrine this branch rests on deliberately split the epistemic question (settled: machine observation counts) from the authorization question (unsettled, founder-owned). No repository spec, no interview with outsiders, and no measurement can produce the founder's authorization policy; the founder stating it IS the datum.

**What each answer would mean.** "Yes" makes the gate-parity solution's full value real and hands the autonomy-ledger branch its starting scope. "No, per-instance sign-off stays" reduces this solution to removing retyping ceremony — still worth having, but the sibling solutions queued behind it should be re-costed. A scoped "yes-for-some-classes" is the expected answer and becomes the first entry of the autonomy ledger.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The founder is the measurement: the doctrine left the authorization question founder-owned, and only their stated policy answers it.
