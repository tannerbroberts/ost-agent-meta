#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Count what share of observed path failures came through this product's own tools]]

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Enough of the observed path failures arrive through tools this repository controls that improving their error messages would cover most of the friction.

**Why it is the riskiest one.** The solution's own limit section concedes the doubt, and the parent opportunity's census is what makes it sharp: the failures it lists are `sed`, `ls`, a shell glob, and `git`. Not one of those is this product's to change. If the corpus is dominated by shell tools, this solution improves the error surface of a minority of calls while the majority keep returning `No such file or directory` — and it would be the cheapest of its three siblings mainly because it does the least.

**What class this is.** Feasibility, and it is settled by counting, not by asking. The evidence is the transcript corpus already on disk.

**How it could come out false — usefully.** A split result would redirect the solution rather than kill it: if the product's own MCP tools are a small share of path failures but a large share of *expensive* ones, the claim narrows to those and stays worth building. The count is what tells the difference, and nobody has taken it.
