#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Deliver three free trees and count how many the recipient shows to someone else]]

Approach teams outside the network directly with a concrete offer: hand over your existing customer evidence and get back a maintained tree, free, with no obligation. The reach comes from what they do with it afterwards — a PM who takes a useful tree into a roadmap review is showing it to people the author will never meet.

The offer doubles as the sharpest available test of demand. A team that will not hand over real evidence for free has told you something that no amount of publishing or listing ever will.

**Compared to the alternatives.** The only route that produces both distribution and evidence at once, and the only one where the author learns something about strangers rather than broadcasting at them. It is also the most labour-intensive per contact by a wide margin, does not scale past a handful, and depends entirely on the first few trees being genuinely good.

**What would make this the wrong pick.** Free work that lands with a busy team often goes unread, and an unread tree travels nowhere. The mechanism depends on the recipient caring enough to show someone, which is the step furthest outside anyone's control.

Approaching teams and interpreting what they say is a human's work. No pass may do it or record its result.
