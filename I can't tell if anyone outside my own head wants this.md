#Opportunity #unvalidated #needs-customer-interview
[[I can't tell which evidence deserves weight]]
[[What the agent struggles with every session disappears]]
[[Concierge design-partner cohort]]
[[Instrumented public trial with a willingness-to-pay probe]]

**The need (customer's voice):** "Everything I know about whether this product is wanted comes from me or from a model agreeing with me. I have no signal from a person who isn't already invested in it being a good idea."

**Why it matters:** Today there are exactly two input channels — the founder, and the agent's own dogfooding friction. Neither is an outside user. The highest-signal evidence about demand (willingness to pay, retention, repeat use) is entirely absent, so every downstream decision inherits the believability of one person's opinion.

**Litmus test (is this an opportunity, not a solution?):** Many distinct ways to address it — recruit design partners, instrument a public trial, run story-based interviews with teams already doing discovery, sell before building, observe an adjacent community. Passes.

**Provenance caveat:** Founder-stated, not sourced from a story-based customer interview. It is a hypothesis about a need, not a validated need. A human should confirm or discard it against real customer conversations.
