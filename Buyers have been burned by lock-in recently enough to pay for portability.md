[[Ask ten buyers what happened the last time a tool they relied on shut down]]

Portability may be a real advantage nobody pays for, which is a different failure from not having one. The question is whether buyers have a live memory of losing something to a shutdown — because they do not price this until they need it, and by then they are not shopping.
