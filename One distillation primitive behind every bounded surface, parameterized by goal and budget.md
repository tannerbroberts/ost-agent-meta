#Solution #compression #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

Replace the per-surface, hand-carved caps with a single primitive — distill(source, budget, goal) — that the rollup, the excerpts, the briefing and every future sense call. The budget is on bytes entering the context (the resource actually being protected); the goal term defines what counts as signal, and the OST supplies it for free, because the root Outcome node is the goal. Once one primitive stands behind every surface, an improvement to compression lands everywhere at once instead of being re-carved per surface, and a newly commissioned sense gets compression on day one instead of after its first injury.
