#Solution #founder-theory #self-organization #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

When a node is found to rest on a misunderstanding, it is not erased and not left standing as-is — it is redrawn as a closed road: kept on the map, marked with what was believed, what turned out to be true, and what the correction cost. "Justify all data by weaving misconceptions into the map that gets built, but legibly" (founder, 2026-08-10). A map that hides its old wrong turns sends every future traveler down them again; a map that leaves them drawn as open roads does the same. The closed-road marking is the third state, and it is *evidence* — the strongest kind the tree can generate about itself, since a documented misconception is an observed self-failure, which the mandate says outranks every other node here.

**Contrast with siblings:** the other two are motion mechanisms (one node, many nodes); this one is the rendering discipline that makes the aftermath of motion honest. It is also the direct answer to the founder's open question — what to do with a mis-construed opportunity: neither delete it nor leave it, but convert it into a warning with coordinates.
