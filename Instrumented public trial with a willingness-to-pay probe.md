#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Pre-order probe - will anyone pay before the map proves itself]]

Open a self-serve trial that anyone can start, instrument what they actually do (first tree created, evidence ingested, return visits, passes completed), and place a real price in the path — a paid tier or pre-order — so that money and retention become observable rather than asserted.

**How it differs from its siblings:** the only sibling that produces the two highest-signal data types named in the outcome — willingness to pay and retention — and the only one whose evidence volume grows without founder time.

**Trade-off:** needs the product to stand up unattended for strangers, and a trickle of unqualified sign-ups can look like demand while telling you nothing.

**Riskiest assumptions to test:** that enough qualified people will arrive to read any signal at all (desirability); that anyone will pay before the map has proven itself (viability); that a stranger can get to first value without the founder (usability).
