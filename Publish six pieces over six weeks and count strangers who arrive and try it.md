#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that publishing the work reaches strangers rather than the existing network. If everyone who arrives is already a contact, this is not distribution and the opportunity is untouched.

**Risk category: viability.**

**Design.** Publish six pieces over six weeks, each built from work already being done — a tree, an assumption test and what it returned, a killed idea. For every arrival, determine whether they are reachable through the existing network or not. Count only strangers, and among those count how many install or ask a real question.

**Why it is small.** The material is a by-product of discovery already happening; the extra cost is the writing.

**What it will not cover.** Six weeks is short for something that compounds slowly, so a null result is weak evidence against. It also cannot separate people who came for the method from people who came for the tool, and the first group will not buy anything.

A human runs this and records the result.
