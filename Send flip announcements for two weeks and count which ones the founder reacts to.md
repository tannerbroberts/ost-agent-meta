#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

Small and fast: for two weeks, whenever a post-build observation records a red-to-green flip, a human relays one short announcement to the channel the founder already watches — no build needed, a manual relay is the test. Count reactions. This probes only the tuning-out belief; it says nothing about whether flips are the highlights the founder most wants (the digest test beside this probes content), and a two-week founder-of-one sample generalizes to nobody else.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: The founder's own attention is the measurement — only he can read, ignore, or mute the announcements, and only his reactions say whether the signal survived.
