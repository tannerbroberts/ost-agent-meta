[[Author–judge separation is checkable from the vault's own record]]
[[A machine-cleared permit may start work without per-instance founder sign-off]]

Retire the human-recorded-result requirement from `ost-agent gate` for claims whose deciding reality is machine-readable: a valid instrument's verdict clears the worth-building permit with no human retyping what a program already observed. Founder doctrine (2026-08-10): human senses are gene-survival-tuned instruments, nothing magical — "programs can observe reality with even MORE high fidelity than a human can" — and the tree should stop faking incapability. Humans are flagged for what is genuinely missing (authorization, human-resident phenomena like demand and trust, a sense nobody has built yet); they are never a universal notary.

**What must replace the human, named at birth:** the human in the gate was a counterparty against self-grading, not a sensor. This solution replaces that function with instrument-validity discipline rather than deleting it: a verdict counts only from an instrument that was red for a reason specific to its question (`no-spec` redness grants nothing — the vault's own record of 260/266 vacuous reds is the cautionary dataset), and the party that authored the claim is not the sole author of its judge. If validity discipline is dropped rather than substituted, this becomes the self-grading failure the mandate exists to catch, wearing parity as a justification.

**Contrast with siblings:** unattended feasibility tests generate machine observations; the proof lane derives truth without observation; the autonomy ledger converts verdicts into scope. This one changes what the *gate* accepts — it is the doorway the other three limbs' verdicts walk through, and without it their outputs still queue behind a human.

**Scope boundary:** this clears the epistemic half only. Whether a human must still authorize what gets built and spent is a separate founder-owned question, deliberately not answered here.

**Provenance caveat:** founder doctrine, spoken 2026-08-10, mapped at the founder's direction. Floor rung (`assertion`).
