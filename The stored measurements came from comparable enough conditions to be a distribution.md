#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Replay the stored measurements and check whether they came from comparable conditions]]

A history-based gate is only as good as its history's provenance. If the measurements mix machines and load conditions, the distribution is noise with a confidence interval drawn around it — and the gate goes quietest exactly when the data is worst.
