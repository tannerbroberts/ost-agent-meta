[[The operator grades the first ten differentia statements as discriminators or filler]]

**Kind: usability.** A gate can require a sentence; it cannot judge one. The belief, stated so it can be false: a pass (or human) forced to write "a solution addressing this and not its sibling is …" at creation time writes real discriminators — statements a reader could use to route evidence between the siblings — rather than title-restating filler that satisfies the gate and discriminates nothing. If the filler rate is high, the gate adds friction without adding distinctness, which is worse than no gate.
