[[Test scan confirms no secret ever lands in vault or history]]

Excluding tokens from the blast radius of a compromised vault is only real if the exclusion holds everywhere — git history, a synced remote, a node body. One leaked credential in history is permanent, so the guarantee is all-or-nothing and has to be checkable.
