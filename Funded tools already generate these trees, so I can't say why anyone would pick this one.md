The question a prospect actually asks is why they would not use one of those.

What the scan does not establish is demand for this product — it is evidence that the category exists, which cuts both ways. **The need:** I want a one-sentence answer to "why not one of the funded tools", grounded in something they demonstrably do not do.

More than one way to address this: sharpen the honesty architecture into the stated differentiator, compete on operator ownership and local data, target the segment those tools underserve, or integrate rather than compete on tree-drawing.
