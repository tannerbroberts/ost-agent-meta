#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Send flip announcements for two weeks and count which ones the founder reacts to]]

Desirability. The belief: red-to-green flip announcements arrive rarely enough, and matter enough, that the founder keeps reading and reacting to them instead of muting the channel. Stated so it could be false: the founder could tune them out within a week, the way the tree already suspects operators tune out block notifications ("An operator notified at every block keeps acting on the notifications rather than tuning them out" records the same worry for a different event class). If false, the immediate-announcement solution delivers noise, not reassurance, and the digest or criteria-note alternatives should win the comparison.
