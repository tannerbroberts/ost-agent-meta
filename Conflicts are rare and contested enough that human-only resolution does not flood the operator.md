#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Count how many vault conflicts are mechanical, to see what a human-only rule would actually cost]]

Most conflicts in an append-only Markdown vault are mechanical and safely resolved. A rule treating all of them as human-only sends a great deal of trivia to the operator — and an operator sent enough trivia stops reading any of it.
