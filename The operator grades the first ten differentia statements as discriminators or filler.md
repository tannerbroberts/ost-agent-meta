#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Runs itself once the gate exists:** the first ten creations that trip the differentia requirement produce the sample; the operator reads the ten sentences beside their sibling pairs and grades each usable-or-filler in one sitting.

**Why this bar:** the gate's whole value is preventing entangled siblings cheaper than the decorrelation pass repairs them. A 70% real-discriminator rate keeps that trade; below it, the sweep still has to adjudicate most pairs and the gate has only taxed authoring.

**What this does not settle:** whether differentia-gated trees actually flag fewer extent overlaps over time — that is the downstream effect, measurable from the decorrelation pass's own caseload.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: Whether a sentence really discriminates two needs is the same judgement the extent-flag review asks of a human; a gate or a pass grading its own required prose is self-grading.
