#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Count how many recurring-input artifacts the founder has actually kept current, before asking for another]]

**The belief, stated so it could be false:** the founder edits the manifest when the loop's procedure changes, on roughly the same day, and keeps doing so for as long as the channel matters.

**Risk category: viability.** The mechanism is trivial to build and impossible to make work if this is false.

**How it could turn out false, with evidence already on this tree pointing that way.** This project has an open belief of exactly the same shape beside it — "The founder will actually maintain a highlight criteria note over time" — and its own node observes that every prior artifact asking for recurring founder input is evidence to check that belief against. If manifests go stale here the way they have elsewhere, the failure is silent and points the wrong way: a stale entry keeps suppressing a sequence that has stopped being procedure, so real friction stops arriving and nothing announces it. An unmaintained manifest is worse than no manifest, which is what makes this the belief to test before building rather than after.

**What it does not settle.** It says nothing about whether suppression is the right shape of fix. A founder who maintains the manifest diligently could still be maintaining a mechanism that files the wrong things — that is the sibling candidates' question, not this one.
