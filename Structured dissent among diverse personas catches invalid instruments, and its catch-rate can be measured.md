#Assumption #founder-theory #goal-acquisition #machine-witness #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Seed the dissent panel with planted-defective instruments and measure its catch rate against a pre-committed bar]]

The belief the gate-parity solution depends on, stated so it can be wrong: a debate of diverse personality-prompted judges — each charged with attacking an instrument's validity (does the spec exist, can it pass vacuously, does red mean what the question asks) and with pre-registering what its passing would commit the tree to — reliably rejects invalid instruments that a single author would wave through, AND the panel's reliability is itself measurable by machine.

The second clause is the load-bearing one. Three substrate-level ways this belief fails, named at birth: shared blind spots below the prompt layer that persona diversity cannot reach; orchestrator capture, since whoever authors the personas and aggregates the debate steers it; and dissent collapse, where inter-debater sycophancy produces a panel that never disagrees but still emits verdicts. The founder's own examples (one-distribution, one-incentive model failures expressed through every persona the substrate could wear) are the evidence these are real. Each failure mode is measurable: a canary corpus of planted-defective instruments with a measured catch rate, dissent-rate telemetry that declares a never-dissenting panel dead, frozen pre-registered pass semantics. The synthesis this assumption encodes: unmeasured self-grading is the failure case; a debate whose catch-rate is machine-witnessed is a valid counterparty.

**Natural first test (compute-only):** seed the panel with known-defective instruments — vacuous reds like the vault's own 260/266 "No test files found" episode, tautological passes, mis-scoped specs — alongside valid ones, and measure catch rate against a pre-committed threshold. Its red instrument belongs in the OST-Agent suite via the normal branch/PR flow so main stays green.
