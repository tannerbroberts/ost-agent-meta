#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Risk category: desirability.** Importing buys speed only if striking-what-you-reject produces the same felt ownership as authoring. It could be false: the founder's "felt-out distinction between what they consider to be an acceptable axiom and what they don't" may be exactly the thing a veto pass skips — reading someone else's axiom and not objecting is weaker than having formed it — so the first unwelcome derivation gets answered with "I never really accepted that axiom," and the whole imported set loses standing at once, which is worse than never having had it.
