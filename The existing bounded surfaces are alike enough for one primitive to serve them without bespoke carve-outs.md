#Assumption #compression #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Route the rollup, the excerpts and the briefing through one distill call and prove no surface bypasses it]]

The single-primitive design rests on the belief that the rollup, the excerpt server, the briefing and the capped lists are variations of one operation (rank what matters to the goal, fit it to the budget, name what was hidden) rather than genuinely different operations that only look alike from a distance. If two of them resist the shared shape — needing incompatible ranking, or a budget in a different unit — the primitive grows flags until it is the old per-surface carving with extra indirection. Stated so it could be wrong: at least the three named surfaces can route through one distill(source, budget, goal) with no surface-specific behavior escaping the parameters.
