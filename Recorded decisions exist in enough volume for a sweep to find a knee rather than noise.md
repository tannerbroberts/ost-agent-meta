#Assumption #compression #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Sweep recorded decisions at shrinking budgets and require a measurable knee for at least one surface]]

The sweep needs replayable pairs of (full input, decision actually taken) in enough volume that verdict-flip rates at each budget are signal. The vault has 134+ evidence records and every rollup is recomputable from tree history, so the raw material plausibly exists — but if the recorded decisions are too homogeneous (mostly transcript-friction crumbs of near-identical shape), the curve will be flat everywhere it was measured and the knee will sit in the unmeasured region. Stated so it could be wrong: the existing vault history contains at least enough decision diversity that the fidelity curve has a measurable knee for at least one real surface.
