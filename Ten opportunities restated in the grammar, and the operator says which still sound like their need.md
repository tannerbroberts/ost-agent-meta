#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Cheap and reversible:** pick ten existing opportunities the operator wrote or knows well, restate each in the controlled grammar (situation, struggle, desired progress), and show the pairs side by side. One sitting, no code, and a failed test costs nothing — the originals are untouched.

**Why the bar is higher than the differentia test's:** a differentia is scaffolding the reader consults; a normalized statement would *replace* the operator's words on the map they navigate by. Replacement earns a stricter recognition bar than annotation.

**What this does not settle:** whether normalized statements actually improve duplicate detection — that is measurable mechanically once ten normalized pairs exist to compare against the Jaccard and extent baselines.

A person outside the building is the measurement here: Recognition of one's own need in a rewriting is irreducibly the need-holder's judgement; nobody else can report it.
