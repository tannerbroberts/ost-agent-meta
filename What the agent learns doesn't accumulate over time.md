#Opportunity #unvalidated #needs-customer-interview

**The need (customer's voice):** "It is good at finishing a task today and useless at being smarter about my product six months from now. Every run starts over — I am the only thing carrying continuity, and I forget."

**Why it matters:** Founder theory holds that intelligence is compression: an accurate mapping from actions to world-state change toward a goal. Short-horizon goal completion is already commoditized; the scarce, compounding capability is collecting and *arranging* information over long horizons so that later work stands on earlier work. If knowledge does not accumulate and reorganize itself, the map never gets more believable no matter how much is fed in.

**Litmus test:** Many directions — periodic re-synthesis of the tree, decay/refresh of stale nodes, cross-run memory of what was already tried, structural refactoring proposals as the opportunity space grows. Passes.

**Provenance caveat:** This is founder theory, explicitly labelled observational and sitting at the floor of the believability ladder. It is the most speculative node in the tree and should be the first thing external data either grounds or kills.
