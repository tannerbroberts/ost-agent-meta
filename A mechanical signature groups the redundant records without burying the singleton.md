#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Tool name plus error class is a good enough signature: it collapses the repeating frictions into a handful of clusters while leaving the rare, one-off event individually visible.

**Kind.** Feasibility.

**How it could turn out false.** The two goals pull against each other and a single signature function has to serve both. Too coarse and the `clarifying_question` in session `2a4bcf6e` — one unattended firing stopping to ask a human which of three ways to proceed, the most consequential single event in the whole corpus this pass read — lands in a bucket labelled "AskUserQuestion ×1" beneath forty permission denials and is never opened. Too fine and the permission denials split by tool name into `ost_check`, `ost_status`, `ost_debt`, `ost_flag_humans_required` and `Glob`, which is five clusters describing one problem, and the queue has been reorganised rather than reduced.

**Why it is the riskiest one here.** The solution is cheap and its plumbing is trivial. Everything rests on whether one mechanical rule can be simultaneously coarse enough to reduce and fine enough to preserve — and if it cannot, the cheapest candidate becomes the one that hides the evidence that mattered.
