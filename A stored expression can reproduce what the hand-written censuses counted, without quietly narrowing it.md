#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Feasibility belief, and the one this candidate lives or dies on:** an expression language small enough to be worth building is still rich enough to say what the censuses already on this tree actually counted.

The censuses are the specification, and they are not simple. One splits a single denial string into two populations by the phrase that follows it. One counts occurrences and distinct sessions as separate figures from the same scan. One states outright that its two rows "sum to 125 rather than 127" because other denial shapes were not broken out, and declares both rows floors rather than measurements. Reproducing that faithfully means the language can express a partition, two aggregations over one scan, and an admitted residue.

**The failure that would be invisible.** A language too poor for those does not error — it returns a number. A stored expression that always runs and quietly counts a narrower set than the prose it replaced is worse than the stale prose, because a stale number carries its date and invites distrust, while a live number carries neither. This is the specific way this candidate can look like it succeeded while making the tree less true, and it is why the test beneath it compares against recorded counts rather than merely checking that expressions evaluate.
