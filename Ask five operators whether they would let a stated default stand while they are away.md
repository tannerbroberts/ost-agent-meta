#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

**Desirability, and it is the riskiest assumption here by a distance.** This solution converts not-answering into approval. The mechanism is trivial to build and worthless without the consent, so the thing to find out first is whether operators will give it — and, more usefully, *for what*. Show five operators a real fork from their own run, stated as a default with reasoning, and ask whether they would have let it stand.

The second half matters as much as the first: where the five draw the boundary is the raw material for [[A standing authority contract naming which classes of decision compute may take alone]]. Five refusals that agree on where the line sits is a more useful result than five acceptances that do not.

**Lane: humans-required.**

**What this does not settle.** Nothing mechanical. Stated willingness in an interview is not observed willingness at 3am with money running, and this rung is `stated` at best — an operator who says yes to a hypothetical default may still reach for the kill switch the first time one fires.


A person outside the building is the measurement here: Whether an operator will let their silence authorise a decision is a consent question about the operator's own risk, and only that person can answer it — no exit code confers permission to act on someone's behalf.
