#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Filing corroboration clears the item and moves no rung]]

**Kind: feasibility.**

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** The whole value of this candidate rests on corroboration being a *lighter* act than creating a node — an item leaves the queue, a count rises, and nothing new is claimed. If filing corroboration turns out to mint a node, mutate the target's evidence rung, or otherwise move a gate, then it is node creation wearing a cheaper name, and an unattended pass filing 76 items would have quietly raised the believability of four opportunities without a single new observation behind it.

The rung is the specific danger. Seventy-six transcript records attached to one node must not make that node more believable than one record does: they are one channel observed repeatedly, not independent sources, and the ladder's whole point is that repetition is not corroboration.

**What would make it false.** Filing corroboration changing anything on the target beyond a count and a recorded id — in particular its evidence rung.
