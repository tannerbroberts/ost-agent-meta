#AssumptionTest #unvalidated #evidence/assertion

The assumption is that this buyer wants a record that could contradict them. They may instead want one that supports a decision already made — in which case a tool that refuses to mark things validated is actively unhelpful, and the segment rejects it for the property that makes it good.

**Risk category: desirability.**

**Design.** Ten interviews. Ask about the last roadmap review where someone asked how they knew: what was asked, what they showed, what they wished they had. Then ask what would have happened if their evidence had visibly said "one person said this once". Count how many treat that as useful and how many as a problem to avoid showing.

**Why it is small.** Ten conversations about a specific recent event, which is the kind of question that produces stories rather than opinions.

**What it will not cover.** People describe their reviews as more rigorous than they are, and this question invites that. Asking to see the actual artefact they showed would ground it and is worth adding where anyone will.

A human runs this and records the result.
