#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Attach thirty self-observations to one node and require the rung not to move]]

**The belief, stated so it could be false.** Attaching many transcript records to one opportunity raises how well-sourced it *looks* by exactly as much as it deserves — the actor ceiling holds, so thirty self-observations read as one actor observing itself thirty times and never as thirty independent voices.

**Kind.** Feasibility, with a viability edge: what is at stake is whether the tree's headline believability numbers stay honest.

**How it could turn out false.** The rollup's per-bucket "N source(s)" figure counts sources, and nothing in that line says how many actors they came from. A solution designed to make recurrence count is a solution designed to drive that number up, and the one actor able to drive it is the agent itself. If the count and the rung are read together by a human skimming the rollup — which is exactly what the rollup is for — then a bucket showing "40 source(s), rests on assertion" invites a conclusion the sources do not support. The tree already worries about this in general ("Worry the agent is grading its own homework"); this solution is the specific mechanism that would make it easy.

**Why it is the riskiest one here.** Whether the attachment plumbing works is a small question. Whether it quietly corrupts the one number the operator uses to judge the tree at a glance is the question that decides if this solution should exist.
