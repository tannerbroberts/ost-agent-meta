#Assumption #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[Ideate three times over a map with closed roads and count re-proposals of the closed misconception]]

**Risk category: desirability/usability.** Keeping a corrected mistake on the map is only justified if visibility does work: the next reader — human or ideating agent — sees the closed road and does not propose driving down it again. It could be false: ideation may re-propose the misconception anyway (novelty pressure reads old closures as stale), or the closed roads may accumulate until the map is mostly museum, at which point legibility of the *open* roads is what suffers. If closures don't prevent re-proposal, the directive's "justify all data by weaving misconceptions into the map" costs map clarity and buys nothing.
