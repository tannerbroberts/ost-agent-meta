#Solution #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[A session's narrative and its trace disagree often enough, and materially enough, to be evidence]]

**The idea.** At pass end, mechanically diff what the session's narrative claimed (annotations, reports, counts in prose) against what the trace recorded, and file each material disagreement as a friction event. The disagreement set is the highest-value dogfood evidence there is: it is precisely where self-report fails.

**Contrast with siblings:** the trace (sibling) is the raw record; this is the instrument that turns record-vs-story deltas into filed evidence. Without it, a human has to notice discrepancies by luck — which is how the only known instance (asserted 148 vs actual 123, 2026-07-24) was actually caught.

**Trade-off:** parsing claims out of narrative text is the soft half; false-positive disagreements would erode trust in the channel it feeds.
