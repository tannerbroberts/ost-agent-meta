#Solution #founder-theory #goal-acquisition #axioms #unvalidated #evidence/assertion
[[An axiom's owner will stand by it when a derivation from it bites]]
[[A derived rung can be expressed in the ladder without breaking the fixed order every consumer reads]]

Give proof-shaped truth a rung. The believability ladder ranks classes of *witness* (money, observed, stated, expert, assertion); a machine-checked derivation from declared axioms is not testimony at all, so today it can only enter at `assertion` — a proof priced the same as a guess. The lane: an axiom register where each axiom is declared with what accepting it commits the tree to and who accepted it; derivations from registered axioms that are machine-checkable; and conclusions that carry evidence "proved-given-axioms" — believable exactly as far as the axioms, and marked so the dependency is legible. The founder's key observation is that the felt-out distinction between an acceptable and unacceptable axiom is the human act in mathematics; the register makes that act explicit, human-owned, and reusable, while the deriving is compute.

**Contrast with siblings:** the empirical limb asks the environment; this limb asks the rules. It is the only sibling that lets reality prove things to the agent with no customer available at all — the founder's stated frustration — and it extends the 2026-07-24 cost-curve bifurcation (machine-readable vs human-resident feedback) with a third, derivable territory.

**Provenance caveat:** founder-stated theory (2026-08-10), floor rung (`assertion`). Whether the ladder itself may be extended is a doctrine question for the founder, and this node proposes rather than assumes it.
